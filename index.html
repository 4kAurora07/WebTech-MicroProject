AI Student Performance Analytics Dashboard

Track. Analyze. Improve.

A simple and easy-to-use platform for teachers and students to keep track of marks, calculate grades, and view overall performance. Built for Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences.

Explore Features

Our Key Features

Here are the three main things this dashboard can do:

✏️

Marks Entry

Teachers can enter student marks for each subject easily. Supports internal marks, assignments, and final exam scores.

  • Enter marks by subject
  • Edit marks anytime
  • Save records quickly
🧮

Grade Calculation

The system automatically calculates grades based on the marks entered. It follows the standard grading rules of the university.

  • Auto grade calculation
  • Shows CGPA and SGPA
  • Letter grade display
📊

Performance Overview

View how students are performing overall. See which subjects need improvement and which students are doing well or struggling.

  • Overall performance view
  • Subject-wise breakdown
  • Compare student results
