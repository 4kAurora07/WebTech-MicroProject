AI Student Performance Analytics Dashboard

Track. Analyze. Improve.

A simple and easy-to-use platform for teachers and students to keep track of marks, calculate grades, and view overall performance. Built for Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences as a 1st Year Web Technology microproject.

View Demo Dashboard

Our Key Features

Here are the main features of our student analytics project:

✏️

Marks Entry

Teachers can record student marks for each subject easily. Supports internal assessments, model exams, and lab scores.

  • Enter marks by subject
  • Simple form inputs
  • Keeps clean student records
🧮

Grade Calculation

Automatically calculates grades and GPA based on marks entered. Follows the standard UGC 10-point scale of the university.

  • Auto grade calculation (O, A+, A, B, etc.)
  • Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)
  • Calculates pass and fail status
📊

Performance Overview

View how students are performing overall. See which subjects need improvement and which students are struggling with attendance.

  • Visual progress bars
  • Subject-wise mark comparison
  • Identifies students who need extra help
