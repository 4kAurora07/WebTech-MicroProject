🌟 Project Vision & Purpose

The AI Student Performance Analytics Dashboard is an academic decision-support system developed for Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences. In contemporary higher education, early identification of learning impediments is vital. This platform bridges traditional continuous assessment tracking with predictive analytics, enabling professors to shift from reactive grading to proactive student mentorship.

By correlating continuous assessment scores (CIA-1, CIA-2), laboratory performance, and statutory attendance thresholds, the system automatically computes CGPA projections, visualizes cohort distributions, and highlights at-risk learners well before final semester examinations.

⚙️ System Architecture & Workflow

1. Multi-Stream Data Ingestion
Collects student records, internal assessment scores (CIA-1 & CIA-2), assignment grades, and bi-weekly attendance tracking logs.
2. Statistical Normalization & GPA Engine
Normalizes scores across diverse academic subjects, applies credit weightings, and evaluates student standing on the standard UGC 10-point scale.
3. AI Predictive Risk Classification
Multi-variable heuristic flags students exhibiting attendance under 75% or declining internal score trajectories, projecting potential arrear risks.
4. Actionable Faculty Dashboard & Export Hub
Delivers visual dashboards, instant search capabilities, customizable reports, and client-side CSV downloads for academic audit meetings.

💻 Technology Stack

Structure & Layout
Semantic HTML5 with accessibility tags and responsive viewport scaling.
Styling & Theme
Custom Vanilla CSS3 design system with CSS custom properties, grid layouts, and print stylesheets.
Client-Side Logic
Pure ES6+ JavaScript, handling real-time searching, filter pipelines, and client data mutations.
Data Persistence
Browser HTML5 LocalStorage for persisting added demo students across page refreshes.
Data Visualization
Chart.js integration for responsive interactive HTML5 canvas charts.
